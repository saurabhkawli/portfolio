Farming

I love farming. Soil under the fingernails, the smell of the first rain, and the quiet patience of waiting for a seed to decide it's ready.

01 · Why the soil

Farming taught me a patience no codebase ever could. You can't hotfix a harvest. You prepare the soil, you plant, you care for it every single day — and then you trust the season.

🌱

Patience

Nothing worth growing grows overnight. Not crops, not people, not products.

🌧️

Respect

For the land, for the monsoon, and for every farmer who feeds the rest of us.

📡

Possibility

Technology — sensors, data, AI — can make a farmer's hardest days a little easier.

02 · Seed to harvest

Grown by your scroll.

  1. SeedEverything starts small. A seed is just a plan with a shell on.
  2. RootsBefore anything shows above the ground, the roots go deep. Foundations first — always.
  3. SproutThe first leaf breaks the soil. Like the first green test run, it's a small thing that changes everything.
  4. GrowWater, sunlight, care — and repeat. Iterate daily.
  5. HarvestThe season pays you back for every day you showed up.

04 · What I want to build

The smart farm.

Back in college I built an autonomous car that ran on voice commands — and controlling things over the internet has been fun ever since. My aim is to bring that IoT magic to agriculture — sensors in the soil, data in the cloud and AI that warns a farmer before the crop suffers. Here's a simulated preview of the dashboard I dream of.
