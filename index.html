01 — About

I'm Saurabh — a Master of Computer Applications and an SDET who has spent seven years making sure mobile apps never break when it matters. I build automation frameworks from the ground up, weave them into CI/CD pipelines on GitHub and Jenkins, and unleash them across fleets of real devices on BrowserStack and Kobiton.
